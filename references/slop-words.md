# Slop Word Bank

A scan list of words and phrases that signal AI prose, split into two tiers.

**Tier 1, Hard Ban.** Almost always slop. Cut on sight. If you need the idea, rewrite from the underlying fact.

**Tier 2, Flag in Context.** Normal English. Slop only when the word is empty, stacked with other hype, or doing vague work. Keep it when it carries a specific fact. "We reduced load time" is fine. "We reduced friction" is not.

The rules live in [phrases.md](phrases.md) and [structures.md](structures.md). This file is the lookup.
